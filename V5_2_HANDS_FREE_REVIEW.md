# Hands-Free Review — v5.2

The Hands-Free Review section creates a live review track from items that:

- have already been seen; and
- currently have mastery 1–4.

## Playback sequence

1. Hear the Tagalog phrase or example.
2. Get a five-second silent recall window. Repeat the Tagalog aloud and say the English meaning before the answer.
3. Hear the natural English meaning.
4. Continue automatically to the next phrase.

The queue is conservatively limited to approximately two minutes. Due and lower-mastery items come first. If all active items do not fit, **Build next mix** rotates to the next group.

This activity is deliberately non-scoring: passive listening should not increase an item's mastery score. Mastery continues to be earned through retrieval exercises.
