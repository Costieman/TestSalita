# Salita Quest 5.5.10 — Persistent Navigation

## Purpose

This stability release keeps every major destination available while the learner scrolls and removes the laptop-width icon rail that made navigation harder to understand. It also addresses the most common small-desktop overlap risks at normal browser zoom.

## One navigation owner

`desktop-navigation-refinement.js` is the single enhancement owner for desktop and mobile route completion.

It preserves the source navigation shell while adding:

- Badges as a first-class destination;
- Avatar Collection and Avatar Case as a permanent menu action;
- active-route synchronization and `aria-current="page"`;
- an internally scrollable desktop route list;
- Badges and Avatars in the mobile More sheet;
- the existing dedicated Badges view and Badge Chest placement.

The retired desktop collapse button and `desktop-nav-collapsed` state are removed. No browser preference is stored for an icon-only sidebar.

## Desktop behavior

At widths above 860 pixels:

- the sidebar is fixed to the viewport;
- the sidebar fills the available viewport height;
- route labels remain visible;
- the brand stays at the top;
- the route list scrolls independently when the screen is short;
- the sidebar note stays at the bottom when space permits;
- the main content reserves the sidebar width and does not sit underneath it;
- the active destination remains visible inside the route list.

The sidebar therefore follows the learner throughout long pages without covering the page content.

## Mobile behavior

At widths of 860 pixels and below:

- the desktop sidebar is hidden;
- the existing bottom navigation remains fixed;
- the mobile app bar remains sticky;
- More remains available from the persistent mobile navigation;
- the More sheet has its own vertical scrolling;
- Badges and Avatar Collection are included in More.

No route is removed merely because the viewport is short.

## Small-desktop safety

Between 861 and 1180 pixels:

- the full labelled sidebar remains available;
- the top bar and stat groups wrap;
- two-column lesson, Hands-Free, settings and dictionary layouts may collapse to one column;
- dashboard and metric grids use two safe columns;
- headings, controls and modal actions wrap;
- content containers use `min-width: 0`;
- the page clips accidental horizontal overflow;
- media cannot exceed the content width.

The target desktop checks are:

- 1024 × 768 at 100% zoom;
- 1280 × 720 at 100% zoom;
- 1280 × 800 at 100% zoom;
- 1366 × 768 at 100% zoom;
- 1440 × 900 at 100% zoom;
- the same smaller laptop sizes at 125% zoom.

## Offline update boundary

The PWA cache advances from:

```text
salita-quest-v5-5-9-avatar-case-r51
```

to:

```text
salita-quest-v5-5-10-persistent-navigation-r52
```

The existing navigation JavaScript and CSS remain precached. Updating the service worker does not clear learner local storage.

## Learner-state safety

This release does not change:

- course item IDs;
- mastery, due dates or review history;
- XP, coins, streaks or Daily Keys;
- badge ownership or Badge Chest state;
- avatar ownership, equipped avatar, shards or Avatar Case state;
- profile storage, backups or transfer codes.

## Automated acceptance

The persistent-navigation validator checks:

- the complete desktop route contract;
- permanent mobile More destinations;
- one navigation controller;
- removal of the icon-rail collapse path;
- full-height fixed desktop geometry;
- internally scrollable route lists;
- labelled routes at laptop widths;
- mobile hand-off at 860 pixels;
- small-desktop wrapping and shrink safety;
- service-worker cache coverage.

## Manual acceptance before calling the layout visually stable

Test both Tagalog and Bisaya:

1. Scroll every long view and confirm the desktop menu remains available.
2. Confirm every labelled desktop route is visible or reachable by scrolling within the sidebar.
3. Open Badges from the desktop menu.
4. Open Avatar Collection and Avatar Case from the desktop menu.
5. Confirm active-route highlighting follows Home, Learn, Review, Hands-Free, Dictionary, Journey, Challenges, Progress, Badges and Settings.
6. Reduce the browser height and confirm the route list scrolls without moving the page.
7. Test 1024 × 768 and 1280 × 720 at 100% zoom.
8. Test 1024 × 768 and 1280 × 720 at 125% zoom.
9. Confirm the top bar, stats, headings and lesson controls do not overlap.
10. Confirm no horizontal page scrollbar appears during normal use.
11. At phone width, confirm the bottom navigation remains visible after scrolling.
12. Open More and confirm Journey, Challenges, Progress, Badges, Avatars and Settings remain reachable.
13. Confirm the mobile More sheet scrolls on a short phone viewport.
14. Open a modal and confirm navigation does not cover essential modal actions.
15. Update an installed PWA and confirm learner data remains intact.

Automated validation establishes ownership and layout rules. It does not replace real browser screenshots at the target viewport sizes.
