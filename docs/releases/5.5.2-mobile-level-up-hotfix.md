# Salita Quest 5.5.2 — Mobile level-up safety hotfix

This patch addresses level-up and avatar-reward overlays that could continue reopening in the installed phone app after the 5.5.1 browser hotfix.

## Cause

The phone app could retain older script responses under the historic `5.4.21` asset URLs. A queued level-up was also acknowledged only after its full animation completed. If the app was backgrounded, closed or interrupted during the animation, the pending record could remain and replay at the next home-screen render.

## Fixes

- Tagalog and Bisaya now request the current profile, collection and Level Progression runtimes with `5.5.2` cache-busting URLs.
- A mobile level-up safety runtime acknowledges a celebration as soon as its overlay is inserted, rather than waiting until the animation finishes.
- Invalid future levels, stale pending celebrations and level records above the learner's actual current level are repaired.
- The new service worker forces same-origin files to revalidate online before falling back to the offline cache.
- Service-worker cache advances to `salita-quest-v5-5-2-mobile-level-safety-r45`.

## Phone refresh page

`mobile-refresh.html` provides a one-time phone update path. It removes only Cache Storage and outdated service-worker registrations, fetches the current app files and reopens Salita Quest. It does not clear localStorage, learner profiles, course progress, mastery, XP, keys, badges or avatar ownership.

## Validation

Run:

```bash
node scripts/validate-mobile-level-up-hotfix-v552.mjs
node scripts/validate-avatar-progression-v550.mjs
```
